## Overview

Where's the Fun At? is a digital lifestyle and discovery platform I founded and developed to help both Zambians and international visitors discover events, activities, tourist attractions, hidden gems and unique experiences across Zambia.

The platform also aims to create greater visibility for local businesses, event organizers, creatives and tourism operators across all ten provinces.

🌐 **Live website:** [Explore Where's the Fun At?](https://wheres-the-fun-at-lemon.vercel.app)

## The Problem

Zambia has so much to offer, yet information about upcoming events, activities and experiences is often scattered across different social media pages, websites and word-of-mouth recommendations.

This makes discovering new experiences unnecessarily difficult for both locals and international visitors.

## The Solution

Where's the Fun At? brings events and everyday experiences together in one accessible, mobile-friendly platform.

Rather than focusing solely on tourism or event ticketing, the platform combines lifestyle discovery, entertainment, cultural exploration and professional development opportunities.
